Collections - Daily Tasks Organizer

Class TaskOrganizer
    Attribute: LinkedHashMap<Integer, String> tasks
    Attribute: taskIdCounter

    Method: addTask(description)
        taskIdCounter++
        tasks.put(taskIdCounter, description)

    Method: removeTask(taskId)
        tasks.remove(taskId)

    Method: viewTasks()
        For each (id, task) in tasks
            Print id → task
